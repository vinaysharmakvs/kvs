Admissions and age fit

Before you apply.

Which classes are available at Kidsverse?

Kidsverse School Rehan offers Playway, Nursery, LKG, UKG and Grade 1. We also offer after-school classes from UKG to Grade 10 for Kidsverse students and students from other schools.

What age is right for Playway?

Playway is usually suitable for children around 2 to 3 years. Final guidance depends on the child’s comfort, independence and parent discussion.

Can I visit the school before admission?

Yes. We recommend a school visit so parents can see the environment, meet the team and understand the right programme for their child.

Do you offer Grade 1?

Yes. Grade 1 is available with a focus on English, Hindi, Maths, EVS, handwriting, communication, values and regular study habits.

Learning approach

How children learn at Kidsverse.

Is Kidsverse focused only on academics?

No. Academic foundation is important, but we also focus on communication, confidence, manners, creativity, participation, values and independence.

Will my child get individual attention?

Yes. Our approach is personal and parent-friendly. Teachers observe each child’s comfort, pace, confidence and learning readiness.

How do you build speaking confidence?

Children participate in rhymes, circle time, picture talk, show-and-tell, celebrations, story activities and small presentation moments.

Do you give homework to preschool children?

We avoid pressure-based homework for very young children. Parents may receive simple revision or habit-building activities that can be done gently at home.

Safety, routine and after-school

Daily comfort and continued support.

What if my child cries in the beginning?

Some children need time to settle. We use gentle routines, teacher warmth and gradual comfort-building so the child starts feeling safe.

What do after-school classes include?

After-school support includes homework rhythm, concept clarity, discipline, personality development, English speaking, exam preparation, Sainik School exam guidance and Navodaya exam guidance from UKG to Grade 10.

How do parents stay updated?

Parents receive friendly updates about participation, habits, class readiness and areas where the child may need support.

Can I ask questions without feeling pressured?

Yes. You can ask openly. We believe parents should feel comfortable, informed and respected before making a school decision.
