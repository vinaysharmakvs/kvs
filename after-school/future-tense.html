Rules and Structures

Open only the rule you need.

Future SimpleSubject + will + V1
OR Subject + am/is/are going to + V1

Use it for actions that will happen later.

Future ContinuousSubject + will be + Verb-ing

Use it for actions that will be happening at a future time.

Future PerfectSubject + will have + V3

Use it for actions that will be complete before a future time.

Future Perfect ContinuousSubject + will have been + Verb-ing

Use it for actions that will continue up to a future time.
