Rules and Structures

Open only the rule you need.

Past SimpleSubject + V2
OR Subject + was/were + noun/adjective/place

Use it for finished past actions, past facts and past states.

Past ContinuousSubject + was/were + Verb-ing

Use it for actions that were happening at a past time.

Past PerfectSubject + had + V3

Use it when one past action happened before another past action.

Past Perfect ContinuousSubject + had been + Verb-ing

Use it when an action continued before a past time.
