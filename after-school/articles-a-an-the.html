Articles: A, An & The

Learn which small word belongs before a noun.

Articles become simple when children ask three questions: Is it one? Is the sound vowel or consonant? Is it specific?

Three Question Method

Before choosing an article, ask these questions.

01Is it one thing?

Use a or an only with one countable noun.

02What sound starts the word?

Use a before consonant sounds and an before vowel sounds.

03Is it specific?

Use the when both listener and speaker know exactly which noun.

Structure

Use articles before nouns or noun groups.

A / An + Nouna pencil, an orange

Use before one general countable noun.

A / An + Adjective + Nouna small bag, an old chair

The article depends on the sound after it.

The + Specific Nounthe pencil on the table

Use when the noun is clear or already known.

No ArticleI like music. Birds can fly.

Use for general ideas, plural nouns and many uncountable nouns.

Rules and Structures

Open only the rule you need.

Use Aa + consonant sound + singular countable noun

Use a before words that begin with a consonant sound: a ball, a mango, a uniform.

Use Anan + vowel sound + singular countable noun

Use an before words that begin with a vowel sound: an apple, an egg, an hour.

Use Thethe + specific noun

Use the when the noun is clear, already mentioned, unique or known: the moon, the principal, the bag on the chair.

Use No Articleplural / uncountable noun used generally

Do not use a or an with plural nouns or uncountable nouns: Children love games. Water is important.
