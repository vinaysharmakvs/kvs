Reading confidence

Reading is not memorized. It is built patiently.

The Kidsverse Reading Mission helps children grow from recognition to real reading through phonics, picture talk, sound practice, vocabulary, sentence reading and story confidence.

The reading ladder

From first letters to independent stories.

Each step is practised through age-appropriate activities so children do not feel rushed or afraid of reading.

How we build readers

Reading becomes easier when children understand the pattern.

01

Sound awareness

Children listen to sounds, repeat them and connect them with familiar words.

02

Picture talk

Images build vocabulary, observation, storytelling and sentence formation.

03

Blending practice

Children combine sounds slowly before moving into word reading.

04

Reading aloud

Short, joyful reading moments build fluency and confidence.

05

Writing connection

Tracing, copying and simple writing support reading memory.

06

Home partnership

Parents receive simple guidance to continue reading habits at home.
