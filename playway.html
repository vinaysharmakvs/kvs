Kidsverse Playway Programme

A happy first step into school life.

Our Playway programme helps children settle gently, build social comfort, improve speech, explore movement and become comfortable with school routines through joyful play.

Why Playway matters

The first school year should feel safe, bright and exciting.

At age 2 to 3, children are developing language, independence, motor control, emotional expression and social awareness. Kidsverse Playway gives them the right environment to separate gradually, make friends, follow simple routines and enjoy coming to school.

Learning areas

What children experience in Playway.

Each area is designed for attention span, comfort, safety and joyful participation.

01

Language & rhymes

Simple words, picture talk, rhymes, listening habits and early expression.

02

Sensory discovery

Textures, colors, shapes, sorting, water play and safe hands-on exploration.

03

Motor skills

Jumping, balancing, tearing, pasting, scribbling and finger strengthening.

04

Social confidence

Sharing, turn-taking, greetings, circle time and playing near other children.

05

Creative expression

Art, music, pretend play, dance, clay, colors and imagination-led activities.

06

Daily habits

Clean-up routine, sitting time, snack manners, toilet readiness and independence.

A day at Kidsverse

Simple routines that make children feel secure.

Young children learn best when the day feels predictable, warm and playful.

1Welcome & settling

Warm greeting, comfort time and free play.

2Circle time

Rhymes, stories, actions and listening practice.

3Activity zone

Art, sensory task, movement or pretend play.

4Snack & manners

Independence, sharing and hygiene routine.

5Gross motor play

Balance, coordination and guided movement.

6Goodbye routine

Recap, praise and a happy closing ritual.

Parent questions

Common Playway questions.

What is the right age?

Playway is ideal for children around 2 to 3 years, depending on readiness.

Will my child cry initially?

Some children do. We use gentle settling so the child feels secure gradually.

Is there homework?

No pressure-based homework. Parents may receive simple rhymes or habit activities.
