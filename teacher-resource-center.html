Daily Classroom English

One classroom language for every Kidsverse teacher.

Use these short classroom sentences during welcome, circle time, learning time, reading, activity and pack-up moments. This helps children hear and use English naturally every day.

Grade 1

Complete sentences, writing discipline and reading expression.

Classroom Communication

  • Who can answer?
  • Can anyone explain?
  • Tell me in a complete sentence.
  • Think before you answer.
  • Let's discuss.

Student Language

  • May I answer?
  • May I ask a question?
  • Could you please repeat?
  • I agree.
  • I have another idea.
  • Thank you.

Writing Time

  • Write neatly.
  • Leave one finger space.
  • Start with a capital letter.
  • Use full stop.
  • Check your spelling.
  • Read your sentence.

Reading Session

  • Read with expression.
  • Read confidently.
  • Don't rush.
  • Try again.
  • Excellent pronunciation.
All Classes

Positive language every teacher should use.

Classroom Appreciation

  • Brilliant.
  • Outstanding.
  • Fantastic.
  • Excellent effort.
  • Keep trying.
  • Wonderful thinking.
  • Superb reading.
  • I am proud of you.

Replace Negative Language

  • Instead of "Don't shout", say "Let's use our indoor voice."
  • Instead of "Wrong", say "That's a good try. Let's think again."
  • Instead of "Sit properly", say "Let's show our best sitting position."
  • Instead of "Hurry up", say "Take your time, but keep moving."
