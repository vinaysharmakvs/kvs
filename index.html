Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents ask about Kidsverse School.

What makes Kidsverse School different?

Kidsverse School is known for excellence, modern learning methods, limited enrollments, and a strong parent-first approach. We focus on quality education, confidence building, and holistic child development in a safe and nurturing environment.

Who are the founders of Kidsverse School?

Kidsverse School is run by Neha Sharma and Vinay Sharma, professionals who have previously worked with organizations like TCS, HCL, and UIDAI (Aadhaar). Their experience brings professionalism, innovation, and modern educational practices into early childhood learning.

How long has Kidsverse School been operating?

Kidsverse School has been successfully serving families for the past 5 years with a strong reputation for quality education and caring mentorship.

What age groups do you cater to?

We offer programs for Playway, Nursery, L.K.G, U.K.G, and Grade 1. Our programs are designed to support early learning, creativity, communication, and confidence building.

What teaching approach do you follow?

We use activity-based and technology-enabled learning focused on creativity, communication, confidence, hands-on experiences, and interactive classroom learning. Our goal is to make learning joyful and meaningful.

What is your app-based learning system?

Parents stay connected through our app-based learning experience with updates on activities, attendance, announcements, progress tracking, and communication. This creates transparency and better parent-school interaction.

How is technology used in classrooms?

We use modern educational technologies and interactive teaching methods to create engaging and future-ready classroom experiences for children.

Why do you keep limited enrollments?

We believe quality matters more than quantity. Limited admissions help us provide personalized attention, better classroom experiences, stronger parent communication, and premium learning quality.

Do you conduct admission campaigns?

No. We do not believe in door-to-door admission campaigns. We encourage parents to visit the school, experience the environment, and speak directly with the founders before making decisions.

Can parents interact with the founders?

Yes. Parents are always welcome to meet Neha Sharma and Vinay Sharma, who are actively involved, approachable, and committed to every child’s growth.

How do you ensure child safety and care?

We maintain a secure environment, attentive staff, hygienic classrooms, child-friendly infrastructure, and close supervision. Child safety and emotional well-being are our top priorities.

What kind of staff does Kidsverse School have?

Our dedicated and caring staff focuses on academics, emotional development, communication skills, discipline, and confidence building. We believe every child deserves personal attention and encouragement.

What is the future vision of Kidsverse School?

Kidsverse School has a clear long-term vision focused on modern education, technology integration, and strong foundational learning. The founders are also launching Stone Field International School in 2027 for higher grades with a future-focused international learning approach.

Why do parents trust Kidsverse School?

Parents trust us because of founder involvement, quality education, modern learning methods, limited admissions, a caring environment, transparent communication, and a child-first philosophy. We focus on building confident, happy, and future-ready children.
